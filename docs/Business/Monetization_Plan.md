# Monetization & Business Plan - Kawaii Ouroboros

## Recommended Model
**Premium with Demo** (mirrors the original Ouroboros King approach)

- Free demo with limited acts and pieces
- One-time IAP to unlock the full game
- Optional cosmetic DLC packs later

## Alternative Options Considered
1. **Free + Cosmetic Gacha** — Higher revenue potential but risks alienating core audience
2. **Free + Ads** — Between runs only (non-intrusive)
3. **Hybrid** — Demo free, full game paid, cosmetics as IAP

## Pricing Targets
- Full unlock: $6.99 – $9.99
- Cosmetic packs: $2.99 – $4.99

## Marketing Angle
- "The cute anime version of that chess roguelike you loved"
- Strong visual appeal for social media and trailers
- Target audience: Fans of Slay the Spire, Into the Breach, and anime mobile games
