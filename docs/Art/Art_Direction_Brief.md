# Art Direction Brief - Kawaii Ouroboros

## Overall Aesthetic
Cute anime style with strong emphasis on charm, color, and personality. Think "magical girl meets chess" with a soft, pastel-heavy palette.

## Color Palette
- Primary: Soft pinks, lavenders, mint greens, warm creams
- Accent: Gold, cherry blossom pink, sky blue
- Board: Light wood + pastel tile colors that shift per act

## Character Design Guidelines
- Big expressive eyes
- Stylized hair with lots of movement and accessories
- Chibi proportions for lower-tier pieces, more elegant proportions for higher-tier/rare pieces
- Clear silhouette even at small sizes (important for mobile)

## Piece Categories
- **Common pieces**: School uniform + light fantasy elements
- **Rare pieces**: More elaborate outfits, glowing effects, accessories
- **Legendary pieces**: Dramatic designs with transformation potential

## UI Style
- Soft rounded corners
- Subtle glows and sparkles
- Clean, readable fonts with cute flair

**Status**: v3 – Piece tier visual language expanded. Tier 3 pieces should have more elaborate designs and glow effects.
