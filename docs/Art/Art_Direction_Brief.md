# Art Direction Brief - Kawaii Ouroboros

## Overall Aesthetic
Cute anime style with strong emphasis on charm, color, and personality. Think "magical girl meets chess" with a soft, pastel-heavy palette. The game aims for a hand-painted, cel-shaded look: rich 3D boards that feel like physical playsets, and character standees that look like collectible anime cards brought to life.

## Color Palette
- Primary: Soft pinks, lavenders, mint greens, warm creams
- Accent: Gold, cherry blossom pink, sky blue
- Board frames: Dark wood tone so the colorful play surface pops
- Piece side colors: Sky blue for the player, sakura pink for enemies, gold for royal pieces

## Character Design Guidelines
- Big expressive eyes
- Stylized hair with lots of movement and accessories
- Chibi proportions for lower-tier pieces, more elegant proportions for higher-tier/rare pieces
- Clear silhouette even at small sizes (important for mobile)
- Mini portraits are presented as upright standee cards; the background is removed so characters read cleanly against the board

## Piece Categories
- **Common pieces**: School uniform + light fantasy elements
- **Rare pieces**: More elaborate outfits, glowing effects, accessories
- **Legendary pieces**: Dramatic designs with transformation potential

## Board Design
- Each academy has a unique 8×8 board texture with a decorative border.
- The board is rendered as a framed 3D object: raised rails, corner posts, feet, and a soft ground plane underneath.
- The playable area is inset so the frame never hides the pieces while still giving the board weight.

## Piece Presentation
- Rounded card frames with the character portrait on the front.
- Metallic base rim and a colored ground ring for readability.
- Cards billboard toward the camera so the face is always visible.
- Royals get a glowing halo.

## UI Style
- Soft rounded corners
- Subtle glows and sparkles
- Clean, readable fonts with cute flair

**Status**: v4 – 3D board and standee piece presentation implemented; per-academy board themes supported.
